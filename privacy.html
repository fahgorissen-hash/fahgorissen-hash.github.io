Privacy policy for Claude uploads

Last updated: 28 October 2026.

Claude uploads is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, Frank Gorissen.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses the Google Drive API to upload and update lesson files in the owner's own Google Drive.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • It does not access anyone else's Google account.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising or for training AI models.

Removing access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com, under Security, Third-party apps & services.
